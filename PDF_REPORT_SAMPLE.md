### Scenario 1: Candidate Completes All 3 Levels
- **PDF Pages**: 5-6 pages
- **Charts**: All 4 types (gauge, bar, pie, radar)

- **PDF Pages**: 4 pages
- **Charts**: Gauge, bar, pie (no radar)
- **Recommendation**: Based on single level
- **File Size**: ~200-300 KB

### Scenario 3: Candidate Attempts Multiple Times
- **Shows**: Latest attempt per level

- **History**: All sessions stored in database
